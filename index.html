Two live sources, queried in your browser. Hacker News (via Algolia) and The Guardian's open test key are the two news APIs that a static page can call without a server or a secret. The real scoutfeed reads a few dozen RSS feeds on a schedule, scores each story against a long-lived interest profile, and texts the top three.
